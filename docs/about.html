About

Compiled and built by Sean Coughlin.

This site lists the works of Greek (and some Latin) medical authors. For each work it gives the manuscripts that transmit it, the folios where the text stands, and a link to the digitized page where it begins. It also gives the early printed editions with page references, the authoritative modern editions, and translations.

Sources

The data come from work catalogues compiled in 2019–2020 for the Corpus Medicorum Graecorum/Latinorum: one table per author (Hippocrates, Galen and some forty other authors). Manuscript links were checked by hand at the time. Galen's manuscript list was revised in November 2020. Digitization services change their URLs, so some links may have broken since. Links to viewers that have since been retired (the old Laurenziana viewer mss.bmlonline.it, the old Ambrosiana server, the old Oxyrhynchus server, the World Digital Library) were relocated in November 2026 to the libraries’ current viewers, at the start folio where the viewer allows it (view ms. means the link opens the manuscript, not the folio); the original links are kept in the data. Links still without a current viewer, such as British Library manuscripts whose images are offline since the 2023 cyber-attack, are marked old link. Folio references are still valid; find the manuscript in the library’s current viewer or through Pinakes.

MediManus

Each work also lists the manuscripts in which MediManus (Center for Hellenic Studies, Harvard) records it, with MediManus's own folio range and a link to its manuscript page. The data are imported with the permission of CHS and refreshed regularly. Each MediManus witness is compared with the CMG list: ✓ means both give the same start folio, ≈ that they differ by at most one leaf (often only recto/verso notation), new means the manuscript is not in the CMG list, and an orange CMG: f. … means the two sources disagree about where the text begins.

The lists record digitized witnesses with folio-level links. They are not complete censuses. For the full and current manuscript tradition of a work, use Pinakes (IRHT), Biblissima and MediManus (CHS). Each work page links to its Pinakes record where one is known.

Errors found in the source tables (missing shelfmark numbers, reversed folio ranges, links that opened the wrong page, inconsistent spellings) have been corrected from the evidence in the links themselves. The full list of corrections is in the repository.

Galen texts online

Galen works link to their Greek text in Galenus Verbatim (Mera Galeni Folia), which publishes Kühn’s edition and some later editions, matched by TLG number. The critical editions Galenus Verbatim records for each work are listed beside the link.

Every shelfmark is checked against the manuscripts Pinakes lists for the same work (scripts/pinakes_check.py). Where Pinakes lists exactly one manuscript of the same collection whose number differs by one digit and whose text starts on the same folio, the shelfmark is corrected and the link is taken from Pinakes. Shelfmarks that neither Pinakes nor MediManus list for the work are listed for checking by hand in PINAKES-CHECK.md. Under “Also digitized, according to Pinakes”, each work lists the digitized manuscripts Pinakes knows that are in neither the CMG list nor MediManus; these open the manuscript, not the folio.

Sigla. The sigla of the critical editions (CMG, CML and other editions in the CMG viewer) are transcribed from each volume’s conspectus siglorum, found through the viewer’s tables of contents, and checked automatically (scripts/sigla_check.py). Each siglum links to the page of the edition it was read from. On a work’s page, the Siglum column gives the sigla used for that work; the edition’s other manuscripts, digitized or not, follow in a separate table, with a ? where neither Pinakes nor MediManus lists the manuscript for that work. The manuscripts page gives every siglum a manuscript has in any edition. Transcriptions were made by an AI model from zoomed page images; readings it was unsure of, and every failed check, are listed in SIGLA-CHECK.md.

Arabic translations of Galen and Hippocrates, and the Arabic summaries made of them (the Alexandrian Summaries, Ḥunayn ibn Isḥāq, Ibn Zurʿah, Ibn Riḍwān, Maimonides), link to their text in the Digital Corpus for Graeco-Arabic Studies. Galen’s commentaries on De alimento and De humoribus are lost in Greek (the Greek texts printed by Kühn are later forgeries); they are known through Ibn Riḍwān’s Arabic notes, which quote the Hippocratic lemmata, and are listed both as Galen works and under the Hippocratic works. Links to other sites open in a new tab.

Reference numbers

Diels: H. Diels, Die Handschriften der antiken Ärzte (1905–1907). Kibre: P. Kibre, Hippocrates Latinus. Pinakes: work number in Pinakes. TLG: author and work number in the Thesaurus Linguae Graecae. Fichtner: G. Fichtner, Corpus Hippocraticum and Corpus Galenicum bibliographies.

Type

Greek is set in GFS Porson (Greek Font Society, SIL Open Font License), a revival of the Greek type cut for Cambridge University Press after Richard Porson’s hand. Latin and English are set in Libre Baskerville (Pablo Impallari, SIL Open Font License), a transitional roman from the same English tradition. Greek is never set in italic.

Data

The full dataset is a single JSON file, catalogue.json. It is generated from the source tables by scripts/extract.py.
