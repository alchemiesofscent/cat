# Changelog

Each version is published as a release on GitHub. Corrections to the source tables are listed in
[CORRECTIONS.md](CORRECTIONS.md); shelfmarks and links checked against Pinakes in [PINAKES-CHECK.md](PINAKES-CHECK.md);
the sigla review list is [SIGLA-CHECK.md](SIGLA-CHECK.md). The manuscript data from MediManus are refreshed every week.

## v0.8 · 24 Nov 2026
- Sigla of the critical editions: the conspectus siglorum of 77 volumes in the CMG viewer, transcribed and checked against the catalogue, Pinakes and MediManus. Each work shows the sigla of its editions, linked to the page they come from.
- New table under each work: the other manuscripts used in its critical editions, digitized or not.
- The manuscripts page lists every siglum a manuscript has in any edition.
- Manuscript tables: more space between columns; the contents of a manuscript (e.g. "libri I–III") on a line of its own.
- Corrections are no longer marked in the tables; they are listed in CORRECTIONS.md and PINAKES-CHECK.md, and this version history is on the About page.

## v0.7 · 24 Nov 2026
- Every shelfmark is checked against the manuscripts Pinakes lists for the same work; two one-digit slips corrected (Harley 6326 for three Aretaeus works, Par. gr. 2163 for Ars medica), others listed for checking by hand.
- Every link that names its manuscript (Vatican, British Library, Heidelberg) is checked against the shelfmark.
- Aetius I–III: Laur. Plut. 75.12 (the source table had 72.12).
- Shelfmarks listed in numerical order within each city.

## v0.6 · 24 Nov 2026
- Arabic translations and summaries of Galen and Hippocrates, linked to the Digital Corpus for Graeco-Arabic Studies.
- Links to other sites open in a new tab.

## v0.5 · 24 Nov 2026
- Dates: of composition for Galen (chronology tables) and Hippocrates (Craik); of the manuscripts (Pinakes, MediManus); of the authors (Pinakes, Wikipedia, the compiler).
- Works can be ordered by catalogue, by date, and for Galen by Kühn volume and page, for Hippocrates by Littré.
- A filter box on every page; search across the whole catalogue from the front page.
- Editions in the CMG link to the CMG viewer.
- Dead digitization links moved to the libraries' current viewers.

## v0.4 · 24 Nov 2026
- Galen works link to their Greek text in Galenus Verbatim.
- Latin and English set in Libre Baskerville.

## v0.3 · 24 Nov 2026
- Greek set in GFS Porson, never italic.

## v0.2 · 24 Nov 2026
- Errors in the source tables corrected (shelfmarks, folios, links, spellings).
- Manuscripts and start folios from MediManus, compared with the CMG list.

## v0.1 · 24 Nov 2026
- First version: the catalogue of Greek medical works prepared for the CMG (2019–2020), with each manuscript's start folio and a link to its digitization, early printed editions, modern editions and translations.
